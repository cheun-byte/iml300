98.7% of people thought that women should be allowed to compete in e-sports events




When asked:

“Do you believe there should be an option for separate single gendered tournaments and events?”

Responses were a near 50/50 split. The majority was not in favor of there being separate events for women, however, it was a close split.



So, despite the number of female identifying gamers and positive views of women in esports from the community overall, why is it still so under-supported?
✿✿✿

This project is meant to be a web archive of the growth and achievements of women in gaming and Esports! While most competitive sports are separated by women’s and men’s leagues, Esports is one of the few that doesn’t explicitly do so.

However, the space is still extremely male-dominated and has led to the creation of women’s-only tournaments and leagues. While this exposure and dedication to bringing women into Esports is a huge positive, it still acts as a point of separation between men and women in the competitive scene.

Creating this website as a dedication not only to female players but to staff and other roles in the world of competitive gaming would hopefully be a way to bring more attention to women in Esports and encourage future generations to continue in this field!
