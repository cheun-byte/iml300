Hall Of Fame Recent Events















This project is meant to be a web archive of the growth and achievements of women in Esports! While most competitive sports are separated by women’s and men’s leagues, Esports is one of the few that doesn’t explicitly do so.

However, the space is still extremely male-dominated and has led to the creation of women’s-only tournaments and leagues. While this exposure and dedication to bringing women into Esports is a huge positive, it still acts as a point of separation between men and women in the competitive scene.

Creating this website as a dedication not only to female players but to staff and other roles in the world of competitive gaming would hopefully be a way to bring more attention to women in Esports and encourage future generations to continue in this field!
